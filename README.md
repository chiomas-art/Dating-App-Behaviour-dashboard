💘 Dating App Behavior & Match Dynamics Dashboard
Swipes, screens, and digital heartbeats: Decoding the modern psychology of online romance.

✨ The Story Behind the Data
What actually turns a quick digital glance into a blossoming connection—or sends a conversation straight to the graveyard of ignored chats?

Online dating is more than just random swiping; it is a complex ecosystem driven by human psychology, behavioral habits, and algorithmic design. Using a robust dataset of 50,000 user profiles, this interactive dashboard pulls back the curtain on modern romance. Rather than looking at a single platform, it captures universal dating app dynamics: from how screen time and bio lengths impact visibility, to how interest tags, zodiac signs, and message frequency dictate whether a match turns into a real-life date, a ghosting story, or a relationship.

🚀 What’s Inside the Dashboard?
The application is structured into an immersive analytical journey through the digital dating world:

📊 User Demographics & Swipe Behavior: Analyze how different cohorts—segmented by age, gender, sexual orientation, income brackets, and location types—navigate the app. Look closely at swipe-right ratios and daily app usage labels (from casual checkers to extreme users).

💬 Engagement & Profile Dynamics: Unpack the micro-metrics of attraction. Does having more profile pictures, a longer bio, or a higher emoji usage rate actually increase your mutual matches and likes received?

🎯 The Match Spectrum & Outcomes: Dive deep into the final results of digital interactions. Track how profiles transition across 10 distinct match outcomes—including Instant Matches, Mutual Matches, Chat Ignored, Ghosting, Catfishing, and Relationship Formed.

🛠️ Built With
Frontend & Interactivity: Streamlit

Data Processing & Analysis: Python, Pandas, NumPy

Data Visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn
